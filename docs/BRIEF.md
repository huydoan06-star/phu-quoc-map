## Verified travel facts (with sources)
1. Visa: Foreigners and Vietnamese holding foreign passports may enter and stay in Phú Quốc visa-free for up to 30 days (also when transiting via another Vietnamese international airport/seaport and staying in the transit area before continuing to Phú Quốc). Passport should be valid ≥6 months; you may be asked for proof of onward travel out of Phú Quốc within 30 days. The exemption covers Phú Quốc only — to visit mainland Vietnam you need a visa (immigration on the island may consider issuing one / extensions). Some nationalities have longer nationwide visa exemptions; Vietnam e-visa (up to 90 days) at https://evisa.gov.vn (official).
   Sources: https://vietnamtourism.vn/en/index.php/useful/items/2282 ; https://visitphuquoc.com.vn/en/essential-travel-information/visa-policy ; https://visitphuquoc.com.vn/en/essential-travel-information/faq ; https://vnembassy-riyadh.mofa.gov.vn/en-us/Consular%20Services/Consular%20Procedure/Pages/EVISA.aspx
   Advice wording: "Rules change — always confirm with official sources / your airline before travel."
2. Weather: Best time Nov–Apr (dry, sunny, calm seas). Rainy season May–Oct: rougher seas, short heavy showers. Average temp ~27°C; hottest April–May.
   Sources: https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/mekong-delta/phu-quoc ; https://visitphuquoc.com.vn/en/essential-travel-information ; https://www.vietnam.travel/places-to-go/southern-vietnam/phu-quoc
3. Jellyfish: season April–August (Kiên Giang Marine Protected Area). Higher risk after rain or at high tide; beaches mentioned: Gành Dầu, Cửa Cạn, Bãi Thơm, Hàm Ninh, An Thới. Advice: long-sleeved swimwear, avoid sea after rain or if jellyfish/unusual foam visible, don't touch even dead jellyfish; if stung don't rub, rinse with seawater (not fresh water), vinegar, remove stingers with a flat object, seek medical help if breathing difficulty, dizziness or severe pain.
   Sources: https://news.tuoitre.vn/vietnams-phu-quoc-issues-jellyfish-warning-after-foreign-tourist-gets-stung-103250505151201646.htm ; https://vtv9.vtv.vn/khuyen-cao-du-khach-tam-bien-phu-quoc-vao-mua-sua
4. Sea safety: during the rainy/southwest-monsoon season seas are rougher — swim only at patrolled beaches, follow lifeguard flags. (source: vietnam.travel page above for "rougher seas"). Keep wording general.
5. Sun/heat: hottest April–May; general advice to hydrate, use sun protection, avoid midday sun (general health advice — mark as general advice, not a sourced statistic).
6. Getting around: Grab (GrabCar/GrabBike) operates in Phú Quốc incl. airport transfers, price shown upfront in the app. Metered taxis also exist — make sure meter is on or agree price first. No fare numbers.
   Source: https://www.grab.com/global/airport-rides/phu-quoc-international-airport/
7. Money: currency is Vietnamese Dong (VND); USD may be accepted at some hotels/resorts but everyday transactions are in VND; cards widely accepted at major tourist spots, restaurants, malls. Carry some cash for markets/small shops (general advice). ATM details: TODO (unverified).
   Source: https://visitphuquoc.com.vn/en/essential-travel-information
8. Emergency numbers: Police 113, Fire 114, Ambulance 115. Source: same page.
9. Power: 220V 50Hz; plug types A, C, G (C most common). Source: same page.
10. Local rules: smoking banned in enclosed public spaces (malls, restaurants, air-conditioned areas) — smoke only in designated areas (source: same visitphuquoc page). E-cigarettes & heated tobacco are banned in Vietnam since 1 Jan 2025 (use, carrying, import) — tourists not exempt, fines apply.
    Sources: https://www.tobaccocontrollaws.org/laws/resolution-no-173-2024-qh15-viet-nam ; https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-to-impose-fines-for-e-cigarette-use-and-possession-2478396.html
    Tipping not mandatory (same visitphuquoc page). Dress modestly at temples/shrines like Dinh Cậu: general etiquette — mark "general advice".
11. Language: Vietnamese primary; English widely used in tourist areas; many tourism staff speak Chinese, Korean or Russian. (same page)
12. Admin: Since 1 July 2025 Phú Quốc is a "special zone" (đặc khu) of An Giang province (formerly Kiên Giang). Source: https://visitphuquoc.com.vn/en/phu-quoc-officially-becomes-a-special-zone-igniting-a-new-growth-engine
13. Tourist hotline listed by visitphuquoc: 1900 1720 (from overseas +84 1900 1720).
