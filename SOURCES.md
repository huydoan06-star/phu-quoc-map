## Language choice — visitor market data
- Vietnam 2025: ~21.2 million international arrivals. Top 10 markets: China 5.282M, South Korea 4.331M, Taiwan 1.232M, USA 849k, Japan 814k, India 746k, Russia 690k, Cambodia 687k, Malaysia 574k, Australia 548k.
  - Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (data from the National Statistics Office): https://thongke.tourism.vn/index.php/news/items/354
  - Government portal: https://en.baochinhphu.vn/foreign-arrivals-soar-to-record-high-in-2025-111260106100233228.htm
- Phú Quốc 2025: ~8.1M visitors incl. >1.8M foreign; South Korea and India together >50% of foreign arrivals: https://en.vietnamplus.vn/phu-quoc-targets-top-three-among-korean-outbound-spots-post337574.vnp
- Phú Quốc H1 2025: Korea led international arrivals; growth also from Taiwan, China, Kazakhstan (Phú Quốc airport border police via Tuổi Trẻ): https://news.tuoitre.vn/vietnams-phu-quoc-island-enchants-south-korean-travelers-103250709170638458.htm
- India → Phú Quốc 2025: ~150,000 arrivals: https://www.vietnam.vn/tai-sao-nhieu-khach-an-do-o-phu-quoc

**Decision:** en (default; also US, Australia, India, Malaysia, Europe), vi, ko, zh-CN (China), zh-TW (Taiwan), ja, hi (India), ru (Russia, Kazakhstan/CIS). Not yet: fr/de (no single EU country besides Russia in Vietnam's 2025 top 10), ms, km.
